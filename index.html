About the Firm

Established in Johannesburg, Rhulani Baloyi Inc. Attorneys has built a reputation for delivering results in civil litigation, family law, labour disputes, and bail applications. Founded on the principle that exceptional legal work requires truly understanding your situation.

We serve clients across Gauteng province and beyond, combining deep legal expertise with genuine client care. Our team of dedicated attorneys brings strategic thinking and vigorous courtroom advocacy to every matter, whether it's a complex commercial dispute or a time-critical bail application.

What sets us apart? We listen, we explain clearly, and we genuinely care about outcomes. We've earned recognition as a "good place for growth" with a "friendly environment" and "good care of clients"—testimonials that matter to us.
