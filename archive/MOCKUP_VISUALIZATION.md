## BEFORE & AFTER: How Client Journey Looks

```
BEFORE WEBSITE: Old Way
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Client: "I need a lawyer"
   ↓
Google search → Lucky finding business card (if any)
   ↓
No info about services or experience
   ↓
Call and hope someone picks up
   ↓
If no pickup → Message friends → Word of mouth
   ↓
Finally meet attorney
   ↓
Uncertainty: "Are they good? Do they handle my case?"
   ↓
❌ Many potential clients give up


AFTER WEBSITE: New Way
═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Client: "I need a lawyer"
   ↓
Google search → Finds Rhulani Baloyi website
   ↓
✅ Sees beautiful animated website (professional impression)
   ↓
✅ Reads about 6 practice areas (knows they handle it)
   ↓
✅ Sees relevant case scenario (relates to their problem)
   ↓
✅ Reads real client testimonials (builds trust)
   ↓
✅ Sees transport info (knows how to get there)
   ↓
Multiple options:
  → Click phone button → Call immediately
  → Click WhatsApp → Message instantly
  → Fill form → Email inquiry
   ↓
✅ Quick connection established
   ↓
Owner: Responds within 1 hour
   ↓
Client: Feels heard and valued
   ↓
✅ Consultation scheduled
   ↓
✅ Case won, client happy
   ↓
✅ Testimonial added to website
   ↓
Website now shows real social proof → More clients notice


RESULT:
Website acts as 24/7 salesman
• Works while owner sleeps
• Makes great first impression
• Filters inquiries to relevant cases
• Converts curious visitors to clients
```
