4. Auto-scrolled to contact form → Fills details
5. Clicks SUBMIT → Gets success message

1. Panicked - Needs lawyer NOW → Sees navbar
2. Spots 📞 CALL button (always visible) → Clicks immediately
3. Phone app opens → Number ready to dial

1. Scrolls and sees "Common Legal Challenges" section
