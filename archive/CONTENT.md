## Services Page

### Introduction
Each practice area requires different expertise. Here's what we handle and how we approach each one.

---

### [Service 1] Criminal Law

**The Challenge:**
Criminal charges are a turning point. You need a defence that protects both your freedom and your future. This isn't about technicalities—it's about your life.

**What We Do:**
- **Bail Applications**: Represent you at bail hearings with arguments that secure your release while conditions are still negotiable.
- **Case Assessment**: Review evidence, identify weaknesses in the prosecution's case, and build a defence strategy.
- **Court Representation**: Handle hearings, trials, and sentencing with experienced courtroom advocacy.
- **Plea Negotiations**: When a plea is in your interest, we negotiate terms that minimize damage.
- **Appeals**: Challenge convictions or sentences through appellate courts.

**Your Process:**
1. Initial consultation (confidential, privileged)
2. Full case analysis and strategy meeting
3. Regular updates as the case progresses
4. Court representation at all hearings
5. Post-verdict guidance (settlement, sentencing, or appeal)

**Cost Structure:**
Hourly rates with transparent billing. We discuss fees upfront and explain what each phase will cost.

---

### [Service 2] Family Law

**The Challenge:**
Family law isn't just legal—it's deeply personal. You need a lawyer who handles the law skilfully but also recognizes what's really at stake: your life after this resolves.

**What We Do:**
- **Divorce Representation**: Handle all aspects—property division, maintenance, custody—whether contested or uncontested.
- **Custody & Access**: Negotiate arrangements that serve your children's interests and your parental role.
- **Protection Orders**: Fast-track applications for protection from abuse or harassment.
- **Maintenance Disputes**: Pursue or defend maintenance claims with calculations based on both parties' needs and ability.
- **Mediation Support**: When negotiation might work, we facilitate fair agreements.

**Your Process:**
1. Initial consultation to understand your goals and constraints
2. Assessment of what's negotiable vs. what requires court
3. Negotiation (when possible) to resolve issues efficiently
4. Court representation if negotiation doesn't work
5. Implementation and adjustment as circumstances change

**Cost Structure:**
Varies by complexity. Uncontested divorces can use fixed fees. Disputed matters use hourly rates. We discuss options upfront.

---

### [Service 3] Civil Litigation

**The Challenge:**
When money or property is in dispute, you need a lawyer who sees the bigger picture—not just the law, but the business sense of resolution.

**What We Do:**
- **Contract Disputes**: Enforce or defend contract obligations, from non-payment to breach of service.
- **Commercial Disagreements**: Handle complex business disputes, shareholder conflicts, or partnership breakups.
- **Personal Injury Claims**: Pursue damages for injuries caused by negligence—road accidents, workplace injury, professional negligence.
- **Debt Recovery**: Pursue unpaid debts through court processes.
- **Settlement Negotiation**: Before trial, we explore settlement options that avoid the uncertainty of a judgment.

**Your Process:**
1. Case assessment and damage estimation
2. Attempt negotiated settlement
3. If needed, court filing and discovery process
4. Pre-trial motions and positioning
5. Trial representation or settlement finalization
6. Execution of judgment if won

**Cost Structure:**
Hourly billing for most cases. Some cases available on contingency (payment only if we win and collect damages). Discuss options during consultation.

---

### [Service 4] Labour Law & CCMA

**The Challenge:**
Employment disputes pit you against someone—your employer or your employee. These matters are emotional, urgent, and complex. You need representation that understands both the law and the workplace dynamics.

**What We Do:**
- **Unfair Dismissal Claims**: Challenge wrongful terminations and pursue reinstatement or compensation.
- **Discrimination & Harassment**: Represent employees facing discrimination or harassment at work.
- **CCMA Representation**: Handle all aspects of Commission for Conciliation, Mediation and Arbitration (CCMA) cases.
- **Employer Representation**: Defend employers against unfair dismissal or harassment claims.
- **Workplace Injury Claims**: Pursue or defend compensation claims for workplace-related injuries.
- **Severance Negotiations**: When layoffs are necessary, we negotiate fair terms.

**Your Process:**
1. Case assessment and evidence gathering
2. CCMA referral (if applicable)
3. Conciliation attempt
4. Arbitration hearing (if conciliation fails)
5. Implementation of award or appeal

**Cost Structure:**
Hourly rates, often with package pricing for CCMA cases. Employer retainers available for ongoing labour matters.

---

### [Service 5] Contract Drafting

**The Challenge:**
A contract signed today can cause problems for years. You need drafting that's precise, protects your interests, and is still fair enough to hold up.

**What We Do:**
- **Employment Contracts**: Draft contracts that protect employers while remaining compliant with labour law.
- **Service Agreements**: Create clear terms for any service arrangement—consultants, contractors, vendors.
- **Commercial Contracts**: Handle complex commercial arrangements with clear terms and risk allocation.
- **Lease Agreements**: Draft lease terms that protect your asset and define the relationship clearly.
- **Terms & Conditions**: Build T&Cs for online services or business operations.
- **Contract Review**: Have existing contracts reviewed before you sign.

**Your Process:**
1. Detailed brief of what the contract needs to achieve
2. Draft preparation (standard templates + customization)
3. Your review and feedback
4. Revision rounds as needed
5. Final review and readiness to sign

**Cost Structure:**
Fixed fees based on contract complexity. Simple agreements cost less; complex commercial contracts cost more. All explained upfront.

---

### [Service 6] Bail Applications

**The Challenge:**
When someone has been arrested, bail is often the only way to get them out while the case proceeds. These applications are time-sensitive. Every hour matters.

**What We Do:**
- **Urgent Bail Hearings**: Get into court fast with compelling arguments for release.
- **Bail Condition Negotiation**: Work with prosecutors to negotiate reasonable conditions.
- **Bail Revocation Defence**: If bail was revoked, we fight to restore it.
- **Remand vs. Release Analysis**: Give honest assessment of realistic outcomes.

**Your Process:**
1. Immediate consultation (often by phone)
2. We file court application same day when possible
3. Appearance at bail hearing(s)
4. Negotiation of conditions if bail is granted
5. Release facilitation

**Cost Structure:**
Flat fee for urgent bail applications. Includes the hearing and initial representation. Additional court appearances billed separately.
