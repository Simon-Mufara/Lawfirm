### 2. BAIL APPLICATIONS

**Current:**
"Experienced advocacy in bail proceedings to protect your freedom and rights at critical moments"

**IMPROVED:**

A bail hearing often marks the most critical moment in a criminal matter. It's where your freedom is decided—sometimes within hours of arrest. You need attorneys who understand bail law inside out, who know the judges, and who can present a compelling case for your release.

We've handled hundreds of bail applications across all levels of seriousness. We know the arguments that work. We understand how to present evidence effectively, address concerns about flight risk or danger to the community, and negotiate with prosecutors. We're prepared to go to court with a strong application if negotiation doesn't yield fair conditions.

Time is everything in bail proceedings. We respond quickly, we prepare thoroughly, and we understand that every hour counts.

---

### 3. FAMILY LAW

**Current:**
"Compassionate guidance through divorces, custody matters, and protection orders with dignity"

**IMPROVED:**

Family law isn't about winning—it's about protecting what matters most to you and your children while closing one chapter and moving toward the next.

Divorce is complicated. There are legal questions about property division and maintenance, but there are also deeply personal questions about custody, parenting plans, and your financial future. We've guided dozens of clients through this process with a clear head and a genuine commitment to protecting their interests and their dignity.

We handle uncontested divorces efficiently (simple, affordable, no drama) and contested divorces strategically (gathering evidence, negotiating effectively, and going to court when necessary). On custody and access matters, we fight hard for arrangements that truly serve your children's best interests while respecting your parental rights.

Protection orders are serious. Whether you're seeking protection from abuse or defending against an order, you need experienced representation. We understand the burden of proof, the types of evidence courts consider, and the tactics that protect your interests.

### 4. LABOR LAW

**Current:**
"Expert representation before the CCMA and Labour Court for employment disputes and compliance"

**IMPROVED:**

Employment disputes can feel personal—because they are. Whether you're facing an unfair dismissal, dealing with a wage dispute, navigating retrenchment procedures, or fighting discriminatory treatment, you want an attorney who understands labor law and your situation.

We represent both employers (defending claims, ensuring compliance with LRA procedures) and employees (challenging unfair dismissals, enforcing contractual rights). We know CCMA commissioners. We've argued cases before the Labour Court. We understand the technical requirements of labor law and the practical realities of workplace disputes.

We're direct in our advice: some cases are strong, some aren't, and some settle for good reasons. We'll give you an honest assessment and guide you toward decisions that make sense for your situation.

### 5. CONTRACTS & DRAFTING

**Current:**
"Precision drafting and review of commercial agreements protecting your interests"

**IMPROVED:**

A well-drafted contract prevents disputes. A poorly drafted contract invites them.

We draft and review commercial agreements for businesses and individuals. Employment contracts, shareholder agreements, service agreements, lease terms, business partnerships—we get the language right, anticipate problems, and protect your interests without creating unnecessary complexity or unreasonable terms for the other party.

We also review contracts before you sign them. Sometimes it's a standard template that looks fine but has buried risks. Sometimes it's a complex commercial agreement that needs careful attention. We help you understand the real obligations and consequences before you commit.

Our philosophy is practical: clear language, fair terms, and contracts that actually work in the real world.

## 🎯 TONE GUIDELINES FOR ALL COPY

**DO:**
- Use active voice ("We fight for your interests" not "Your interests will be fought for")
- Use specific examples ("We've handled 50+ custody disputes" not "We have extensive experience")
- Address the reader directly ("You need..." "You deserve..." "We understand you're facing...")
- Show understanding of their situation ("Divorce is complicated..." "Employment disputes feel personal...")
- Be honest about limitations ("Some cases are stronger than others...")

**DON'T:**
- Use legal jargon unless necessary (and explain it if you do)
- Make unrealistic promises ("We guarantee success")
- Sound overly corporate or robotic
- Use clichés ("We go the extra mile," "We leave no stone unturned")
- Emphasize credentials over results (show HOW you help, not just that you're qualified)
